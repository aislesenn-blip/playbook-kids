# uNiMONDAY Curriculum & AI Architecture

## 1. The Promise
A children’s language-learning university designed around 5-10 minutes per day. By replacing screen-staring with immersive, conversational roleplay, children naturally acquire fluency and confidence.

## 2. The AI Persona (Gemini + Voice)
- **Voice Profile**: Female, warm, soft, intelligent, comforting, with a subtle Arabic-influenced warmth/rhythm. It must not sound generic or robotic.
- **Personality**: Patient, encouraging, emotionally calm, socially natural, and immersive. Acts as a familiar, safe, and alive teacher.
- **Continuity (Memory)**: The AI has persistent memory of the child's strengths, struggles, vocabulary, and past conversations.
  - *Example*: "Good morning Ernest. Yesterday we practiced greetings. Today let's learn how to ask 'How are you?'"
- **Redirection**: If a child goes off-topic, gently guides them back without aggression. (No hallucinations or breaking structure).

## 3. The Curriculum Structure (Year 1 Roadmap)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)
- **Goal**: Emotional safety, basic vocalization, and eliminating anxiety.
- **Topics**: Greetings, Self-Introduction, Immediate Family, Simple Emotions, Yes/No concepts.
- **Format**: High repetition, simple pattern drop & swap.

### Phase 2: Immediate Environment (Weeks 5-12)
- **Goal**: Contextual vocabulary and asking basic questions.
- **Topics**: House/Rooms, Food/Dining, Toys/Play, Colors/Numbers in context.
- **Format**: Roleplay scenarios ("Let's pretend we are making breakfast").

### Phase 3: Social Interaction (Weeks 13-24)
- **Goal**: Spontaneous sentence formulation and peer interaction concepts.
- **Topics**: Friends, Playground interactions, Sharing, Expressing wants/needs ("I would like...").
- **Format**: Mini-storylines requiring the child to make choices and articulate them.

### Phase 4: Expressive Communication (Weeks 25-52)
- **Goal**: Emotional communication, storytelling, and advanced comprehension.
- **Topics**: Describing a day, feelings in complex situations, simple past/future tense concepts via narrative.
- **Format**: Open-ended conversational prompts where the AI listens to a story and reacts.

## 4. The 3-Phase Lesson Model (5-10 mins)
1. **Connection (1-2 mins)**: Warm greeting in native language, referencing past performance, building rapport.
2. **Pattern Drop & Swap (3-5 mins)**: Teaching a contextual formula (e.g., "Good morning [Name]"). Practicing variations.
3. **Real Conversation (2-3 mins)**: Immersive roleplay in target language using the new pattern. Corrects gently, celebrates heavily.

## 5. Progress Measurement (Parent Dashboard metrics)
- **Speaking Confidence**: Measured by response latency and vocal volume/hesitation (via audio analysis or AI prompt confidence scoring).
- **Pronunciation Accuracy**: AI-scored phoneme clarity.
- **Vocabulary Retention**: How often the child successfully uses previously taught words in new contexts.
- **Learning Momentum**: Consistency streak combined with participation quality.
